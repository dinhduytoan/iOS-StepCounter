1. Introduction

Walking (“the app”, “we”) helps you track your daily steps and walking goals on iPhone. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used and how you stay in control.
In short: your data stays on your device. We do not collect, sell or share it.

2. Data the app access

Apple Health (HealthKit) data. With your permission, the app only reads your step count. It may also read walking distance, active energy and active minutes to show on the home screen. The app never writes to or modifies anything in Apple Health.

3. How we use data

Data is used only to:

  • show your steps, distance, estimated energy and progress toward your goal;
  • draw 7-day and 30-day history charts, daily averages and your streak;
  • display information in home screen and lock screen widgets;
  • send reminder notifications at the time you choose.

All processing happens locally on your device.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account and do not collect personal information such as name, email, or phone number.
  • We do not send health or activity data off-device.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties.
  • We do not use health data for advertising, marketing or user profiling.
  • We do not use health data to analyze behavior or track you across apps and websites.
  • We do not store health data on iCloud or our servers.

5. Permission you grant

The app requests permission only when needed and explains why before showing the iOS prompt:

  • Apple Health: to read steps and related activity metrics.
  • Motion & Fitness: to update steps in real-time.
  • Notifications: to send walking reminders, if you turn them on.

You can revoke each permission at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices, Settings › Privacy & Security › Motion & Fitness and Settings › Notifications. When a permission is revoked, the app stops showing the related data.

6. Storage and deletion

Your settings are stored on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. Your step data belongs to Apple Health and is managed by you there; deleting Walking does not delete it.

7. Third-party services

The app currently includes no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. If we add any in the future, we will update this policy and update the App Privacy section on the App Store before release.

Optional tips. If you choose to send the developer a tip, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. We do not receive or store your card or payment details, and tips do not unlock any additional features. Apple processes transaction data under Apple's own privacy policy.

8. Security

Because data is processed on your device, its protection depends on iOS safeguards such as your passcode and screen lock. We encourage you to keep iOS up to date and to protect your device with a passcode.

9. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app collects no personal data, this applies equally to all users.

10. Your rights

Because we do not keep your personal data on any server, you control your data directly on your device. If you live where data protection laws such as the GDPR or CCPA apply and have questions about your rights, please contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

11. Health notice

Walking is for tracking everyday activity only and is not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Energy figures are estimates. Talk to your doctor before making significant changes to your activity level.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. For significant changes, we will update the effective date at the top and notify you in the app or in the release notes.
